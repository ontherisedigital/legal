# Cookie Notice

**On the Rise Digital**
Last updated 7 November 2026

**This website sets no cookies of its own.** We use no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts that follow you
around.

Nothing is sold on this website. When you buy something, you do so on the
storefront we link you to, and that storefront sets the cookies needed to run
its checkout and prevent fraud. Those are strictly necessary for a transaction
you asked for, and they are covered by that storefront's own privacy policy,
not by this one.

Our apps set no cookies either — most of them have no browser in them at all.
What each app stores on your own device is set out in the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/).

That is the whole of it. There is no banner to click, because there is nothing
to consent to. If that ever changes, this page changes first.
