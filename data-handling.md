# Data Handling

**On the Rise Digital**
Last updated 8 November 2026

This page answers, in plain terms, the questions testers, reviewers, and platforms ask about how we handle data. The full legal text is in the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy-policy/); if the two ever differ, the Privacy Policy controls.

## What we may ask for or store

Only your **name** and/or **a way to contact you** (usually an email address; for testers, possibly a chat handle or phone number for the tester group). That is all. We do not ask for your date of birth, address, government ID, payment card, location, or device data, and the apps do not collect any of it.

## Where it is kept

- **Nothing is stored in a database** unless you choose to create an optional account in an app that offers one. Without an account, there is no database record about you.
- Otherwise your details live in the email or message you sent us, and in a private tester list kept by On the Rise Digital.
- **Your projects and files stay on your own device.** The apps do not upload them, and we never receive them unless you choose to send a sample.

## Who sees it

Only one person: Alison Risner, the owner of On the Rise Digital. The companies that host the tools we use (for example our email provider, or the database behind an optional account) store the data for us and may not use it for their own purposes.

If you join a tester group chat on a platform such as Facebook, WhatsApp, or Discord, the other members of that group can see what the platform shows them (your name or handle, and what you post). That group is run by the platform, under its own rules.

## What we do not do

- We **do not sell** your data.
- We **do not share** it with third parties for their own use.
- We **do not use it for advertising**, with one exception: if you explicitly sign up to receive email from us, we may email you. You can unsubscribe at any time and we will stop.

## Sales records

Nothing is sold on our website. Purchases happen on the sales channels we use (for example Payhip or an app store), and **we keep the sales information those channels give us** (order, buyer name and email, country, payment status; never card numbers). Each channel also keeps its own records under its own privacy policy and retention period, which we do not control. To have a channel's copy deleted, contact that channel.

## How long we keep it

The periods are set out in the retention table in the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy-policy/): for example, seven years for sales and tax records, two years for support email, and twelve months for unsuccessful tester applications.

Email [admin@ontherisedigitalhq.com](mailto:admin@ontherisedigitalhq.com) to see, correct, or delete what we hold about you. We will respond promptly, and delete it unless we are legally required to keep it (such as sales records). The [Data Deletion]({{ site.baseurl }}/data-deletion/) page explains the steps.
