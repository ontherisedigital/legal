# App Tester Policy and Agreement

**On the Rise Digital**
Last updated 8 November 2026

This is the agreement between Alison Risner, trading as On the Rise Digital ("we," "us"), and each person who accepts an app or app link to test it ("you," "the tester"). **By accepting the app and/or the app link, you agree to these terms.** Please read them before you sign up. Questions are welcome first: admin@ontherisedigitalhq.com.

## 1. Who and how many

We accept 3 to 5 testers per app. Applying does not guarantee a place. You test only the apps you choose and we accept you for.

## 2. What you receive

- **Full access** to each app you are accepted to test, including all PRO features.
- **Lifetime access to that app**, which is yours to keep as long as you keep up your side of this agreement (sections 4 to 7). "Lifetime" means for your lifetime and for that app only. It does not promise that we will develop the app forever or support devices that do not exist yet. If development ends, you keep the last version we supplied.
- **All updates** we make to that app.

## 3. The testing period

- The testing period is **14 days from the day you receive the app or link**.
- You may be granted **one extra 14 days** if you need it. **You must ask for the extension before the original 14 days end.** An extension requested after that point is not available; asking in time is how we know you are taking part.

## 4. What we ask of you

- **Create at least one full project** with each app you test. Use the app the way a real customer would.
- **Give honest feedback.** Criticism is welcome, and a positive review is never required.
- **Give us a reliable way to reach you** (usually email) and keep it working throughout testing.
- **Join the tester group** we set up for announcements and discussion among all testers, such as a Facebook Group, WhatsApp chat, or Discord server, or whatever method we establish with you. If you can't, tell us and we will agree on another method.

## 5. The questionnaire

After the testing period we send a questionnaire. **Return it within 7 days of the date we send it.** We use your answers to improve the app, and we do not publish them with your name without separate permission. The questionnaire is also where you confirm the project you created.

## 6. Staying in touch

Please reply to our messages **within a reasonable amount of time**. If we send you a message and hear nothing in a reasonable time, we may treat you as unresponsive, which is a breach of this agreement. A quick "I'm busy, back on Friday" is a reply. This is separate from the questionnaire, which has its own fixed deadline (section 5).

## 7. Losing access

We may end your access to the app(s) if you:

- opt out of testing;
- do not return the questionnaire within 7 days of it being sent; or
- do not respond to our messages within a reasonable amount of time (section 6).

Ending your access means your license ends, we stop sending you updates, and you agree to stop using the app and delete it. **Continuing to use the app(s) after you have received our request to discontinue use is a breach of this agreement.**

**Selling what you made.** Many of our tools exist to help people create products to sell. Once we have told you of a breach of this agreement, or confirmed that we received your request to opt out, you may not sell, license, or otherwise commercially use anything you created with the app.

## 8. Changes during and after testing

Apps change. We may alter the interface, features, or functionality during testing or afterwards, because of tester feedback or problems we find ourselves. A change might remove or move something you liked. We will tell you about changes through your preferred contact method, which may include a post in the tester group (Facebook Group, WhatsApp chat, or Discord, if we set them up) or another method we have established with you. Suggestions may be declined or scheduled for later.

## 9. Updates and internet access

You get every update we release for the apps you test. Apps obtained through an app store update through the store. **If you got the app through any other sales channel, the app needs an internet connection to receive update notifications and to start the updates.** Open the app online from time to time.

## 10. Your work and your privacy

Your projects and files stay on your device. We do not receive them unless you choose to send a sample, and we use anything you send only to diagnose and fix problems. While your access is active, you may sell products you make with the app if you hold the rights to all content you used (but see section 7 on selling after a breach or opt-out). You may not copy, share, or resell the app, its code, license keys, or bundled assets.

We collect only your name and a way to contact you, use them only to run testing, and do not sell them or use them for advertising. See [Data Handling]({{ site.baseurl }}/data-handling/) and the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy-policy/). Other members of the tester group may see your name or handle and your posts, so don't share anyone else's contact details.

## 11. Honesty if you speak publicly

If you post about the app, say plainly that you received it for testing, for example "I received free lifetime access through the tester program." Opinions must be your own.

## 12. The app is unfinished

Test versions can have bugs. Keep backups of your work. The app is provided as is, and the [End User License Agreement]({{ site.baseurl }}/eula-coloring-book-studio/) applies to your use of it. Ohio law governs this agreement.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes apply to testers who accept after the update, and we will tell current testers of material changes through their preferred contact method.
